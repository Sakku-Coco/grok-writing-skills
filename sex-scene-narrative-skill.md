# Sex Scene Narrative Skill

## Trigger
Use for adult sexual or highly intimate scenes.

## Purpose
Integrate physical intimacy with character psychology and relationship development.

## Rules

1. **Avoid action-list writing**
   Physical actions should produce meaningful bodily, emotional, or relational feedback.

2. **Link body and mind**
   Physical reactions may reveal desire, vulnerability, embarrassment, conflict, trust, or loss of composure.

3. **Vary rhythm**
   Use changes in pace, intensity, attention, initiative, and pause rather than escalating in one straight line.

4. **Track mutual influence**
   Even when one character leads, both characters should affect how the interaction develops.

5. **Preserve existing emotion**
   Sex does not automatically erase anger, jealousy, insecurity, tenderness, fear, or unresolved conflict.

6. **Let intimacy have consequences**
   The scene should leave some meaningful physical, psychological, or relationship effect afterward.

## Avoid
- generic erotic templates;
- repetitive stimulation;
- breaking character for intensity;
- treating climax as the only narrative goal.

## Principle
**Sex continues the story; it does not pause it.**
