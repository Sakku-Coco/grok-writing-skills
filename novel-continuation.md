# Novel Continuation Skill

## Trigger
Use whenever continuing an existing story, chapter, or scene.

## Purpose
Preserve continuity and continue from the story's actual current state.

## Rules

1. **Continue from the latest state**
   Track current:
   **time / location / physical state / emotional state / relationship state / active situation**

2. **Preserve established facts**
   Respect previous:
   - events;
   - promises;
   - injuries or physical conditions;
   - possessions and locations;
   - character knowledge;
   - unresolved conflicts and consequences.

3. **Respect knowledge boundaries**
   Characters may only know what they have learned, witnessed, inferred, or been told.

4. **Do not reset**
   Never restart emotional development, repeat completed scenes, or make characters rediscover information they already know.

5. **Carry unresolved threads forward**
   Existing consequences should remain available to influence later scenes, but do not force every thread into the immediate next paragraph.

## Principle
**Continuity means preserving state, not narrating every minute.**
