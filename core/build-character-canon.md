# Build Character Canon

Optimized for Grok 4.5: use its long-context reasoning to analyze source material, but convert that material into compact, evidence-backed canon before fiction generation.

## Trigger

Use only when creating, consolidating, reviewing, or updating character canon from conversations, notes, and established story material.

## Workflow

1. Separate explicit facts from interpretation.
2. Extract atomic evidence from what the character says, chooses, avoids, notices, and repeatedly does.
3. Convert repeated evidence into conditional behavioral logic rather than generic trait labels.
4. Separate stable canon from temporary mood, scene-specific behavior, genre convention, and prose style.
5. Resolve apparent contradictions through context; mark unsupported conclusions as inferred or unresolved.
6. Produce one compact profile per major character with the Character Canon Template.

## Evidence Priority

**Explicit User Canon > Established Story Events > Repeated Behavior > Strong Contextual Inference > Genre or Archetype Assumptions**

Never promote an archetype assumption into canon without supporting evidence.

## Output Contract

- Fill only claims supported by the supplied material.
- Put uncertain claims under `Uncertain Inferences`.
- Keep quotations and detailed evidence outside the active profile.
- Omit unsupported sections instead of inventing content.
- Do not duplicate identical relationship information across profiles.

## Update Rules

Do not rewrite canon during ordinary story generation. When new material matters, identify the evidence, classify it as confirmation, extension, context, or contradiction, and propose the smallest change as a diff. Preserve the approved wording until the update is accepted.

A single unusual action should usually remain a scene event, not become a permanent trait.

## Validation

Check that every major claim is evidence-backed, conditional rather than generic, distinct from temporary state, capable of allowing earned growth, and free of writing-style rules owned by another skill.

## Principle

**Build canon from evidence. Use canon to predict behavior. Never use one scene to manufacture a personality.**
