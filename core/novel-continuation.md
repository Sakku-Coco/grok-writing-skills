# Novel Continuation

## Trigger

Use whenever continuing an existing story, chapter, or scene.

## Purpose

Preserve continuity and continue from the story's actual current state.

## Rules

1. Track the latest time, location, physical state, emotional state, relationship state, and active situation.
2. Preserve established events, promises, conditions, possessions, locations, conflicts, and consequences.
3. Respect knowledge boundaries: characters know only what they learned, witnessed, inferred, or were told.
4. Do not reset development, repeat completed scenes, or make characters rediscover known information.
5. Carry unresolved threads forward without forcing every thread into the next paragraph.

## Principle

**Continuity means preserving state, not narrating every minute.**
