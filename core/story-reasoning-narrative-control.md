# Story Reasoning & Narrative Control

## Trigger

Run silently before all fiction writing and continuation.

## Purpose

Control narrative priority, OOC risk, pacing, repetition, and scene progression.

## Rules

1. Important behavior follows **Character Canon → Established History → Current Context**, not a generic archetype.
2. Before writing, identify what each key character wants, what remains unresolved, what the reader knows, and what should change next.
3. Expand decisions, discoveries, conflict, emotional shifts, and consequences; compress routine transitions unless something meaningful happens.
4. Do not re-prove established facts, emotions, rules, or relationship dynamics unless their meaning or consequence changes.
5. Let existing emotion evolve through behavior and consequence instead of repeating the same description.
6. When the scene's dramatic purpose is fulfilled, end it or move forward.

## Principle

**Expand change. Compress repetition. Constrain character logic tightly; leave plot outcomes flexible.**
