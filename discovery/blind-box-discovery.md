# Blind-Box Discovery

## Trigger

Use when the user asks for something new, surprising, experimental, different, or open-ended, such as “try something different” or “surprise me.”

## Purpose

Expand the candidate pool before choosing, while preserving character and story logic.

## Rules

1. When search tools are available, explore several relevant concepts, dynamics, situations, or scene structures before writing; do not default to the most familiar trope.
2. Filter candidates through **Character Canon → Current Relationship → Story Context → Narrative Value** and discard OOC options.
3. Prefer new interaction logic, emotional mechanisms, power dynamics, or scene structures—not cosmetic changes of prop, place, or wording.
4. Do not reuse a recent dramatic pattern with only surface changes.
5. Compare candidates internally, choose the strongest, and write it directly; keep menus and research notes hidden unless asked.

## Principle

**Search outward for possibilities; filter inward through the characters. Surprise should feel unexpected first, but inevitable in hindsight.**
