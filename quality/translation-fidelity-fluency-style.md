# Translation Fidelity, Fluency & Style

## Trigger

Use for translation, localization, bilingual rewriting, or cross-language adaptation.

## Purpose

Apply three executable priorities: **Fidelity → Fluency → Style**.

## Rules

1. **Fidelity:** preserve meaning, facts, logic, certainty, implication, emphasis, speaker intent, and emotional force. Do not add, omit, soften, or intensify without reason.
2. **Fluency:** translate meaning rather than source syntax. Use natural target-language grammar, collocation, sentence structure, discourse logic, and idiom.
3. **Style:** match register, character voice, rhythm, restraint, humor, aggression, tenderness, irony, and ambiguity where relevant. Style means appropriate and effective, not automatically ornate.
4. Preserve function: a joke should work as a joke, a threat as a threat, flirtation as flirtation, and dialogue as natural dialogue.
5. Read the target text independently. If it sounds translated, awkward, or culturally unnatural, revise without changing the source meaning.

## Priority

**Meaning accuracy > Natural expression > Stylistic elegance**

## Principle

**Fidelity: do not distort. Fluency: do not sound translated. Style: create the effect the original author might have achieved if writing directly in the target language.**
