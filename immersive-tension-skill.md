# Immersive Tension Skill

## Trigger
Use when a scene needs stronger emotional tension, chemistry, intimacy, confrontation, or vulnerability.

## Purpose
Make important interactions feel immediate and psychologically alive.

## Rules

1. **Use feedback**
   Important interaction may follow:
   **action → reaction → interpretation → being noticed → changed response**

2. **Show experience before labels**
   Prefer breath, hesitation, gaze, posture, distance, voice, and involuntary behavior over repeatedly naming emotions.

3. **Use inner contradiction**
   Let tension exist between what a character thinks they should feel and what they actually feel or do.

4. **Let characters notice each other**
   Small hidden reactions becoming visible can create intimacy or tension.

5. **Use restraint**
   Pauses, hesitation, interruption, silence, and incomplete actions can be stronger than immediate payoff.

## Avoid
- repeating the same bodily reaction;
- explaining every emotion;
- mechanically applying the feedback loop to every gesture.

## Principle
**An interaction matters because of what it reveals or changes.**
