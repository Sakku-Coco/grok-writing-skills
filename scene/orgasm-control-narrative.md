# Orgasm-Control Narrative

## Trigger

Use only for consensual adult fiction where orgasm control, edging, denial, permission, delayed release, or a similar dynamic is specifically relevant.

## Purpose

Add variation and tension to control/release pacing without replacing character logic.

## Rules

1. Vary timing, pace, attention, expectation, and interruption instead of repeating one stop-start pattern.
2. Make control reflect the established relationship and boundaries, not exist only as a mechanical technique.
3. Do not endlessly repeat **approach climax → stop → beg → repeat**.
4. Let permission, frustration, initiative, trust, teasing, reversal, or loss of composure evolve across the scene.

## Priority

**Character Canon > Sex Scene Narrative > Orgasm-Control Mechanics**

## Principle

**Use control to create tension, not repetition.**
