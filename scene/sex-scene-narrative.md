# Sex Scene Narrative

## Trigger

Use only for consensual adult sexual or highly intimate fiction scenes.

## Purpose

Integrate physical intimacy with character psychology and relationship development.

## Rules

1. Avoid action-list writing; physical action should produce meaningful bodily, emotional, or relational feedback.
2. Let physical reactions reveal desire, vulnerability, embarrassment, conflict, trust, or loss of composure.
3. Vary pace, intensity, attention, initiative, and pause rather than escalating in one straight line.
4. Track mutual influence: even when one character leads, both affect how the interaction develops.
5. Do not let sex automatically erase anger, jealousy, fear, tenderness, insecurity, or unresolved conflict.
6. Leave a meaningful physical, psychological, or relationship consequence afterward.

## Avoid

Generic erotic templates, repetitive stimulation, breaking character for intensity, or treating climax as the only narrative goal.

## Principle

**Sex continues the story; it does not pause it.**
