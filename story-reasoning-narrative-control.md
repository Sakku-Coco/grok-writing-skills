# Story Reasoning & Narrative Control Skill

## Trigger
Run silently before **all fiction writing and continuation**.

## Purpose
Control OOC risk, narrative priority, pacing, repetition, and scene progression.

## Rules

1. **Character logic first**
   Important behavior must follow:
   **Character Canon → Established History → Current Context**
   If a reaction could belong unchanged to any generic character of the same archetype, reconsider it.

2. **Know what matters now**
   Before writing, identify:
   - what each key character wants;
   - what remains unresolved;
   - what the reader already knows;
   - what should change next.

3. **Expand change, compress transition**
   Decisions, discoveries, conflict, emotional shifts, and consequences deserve detail.
   Routine eating, washing, walking, resting, dressing, or logistics should usually be summarized unless something meaningful happens.

4. **Do not re-prove established information**
   Once a fact, emotion, rule, or dynamic is clear, do not keep restating it unless its meaning or consequence changes.

5. **Preserve emotion without repeating it**
   Existing emotions should evolve through behavior and consequences rather than being repeatedly described in the same form.

6. **Know when to end**
   Once the scene's dramatic purpose is fulfilled, stop or move forward.
   Do not extend completed scenes with redundant dialogue or domestic filler.

## Principle
**Expand change. Compress repetition.**

**Constrain character logic tightly; leave plot outcomes flexible.**
