# Orgasm-Control Narrative Skill

## Trigger
Use only when orgasm control, edging, denial, permission, delayed release, or similar dynamics are specifically relevant.

## Purpose
Add variation and tension to control/release pacing.

## Rules

1. **Build anticipation through variation**
   Change timing, pace, attention, expectation, and interruption rather than repeating the same stop-start pattern.

2. **Make control relational**
   Control should reflect the characters' established dynamic, not exist only as a mechanical technique.

3. **Avoid repetitive cycles**
   Do not endlessly repeat:
   **approach climax → stop → beg → repeat**

4. **Let the dynamic evolve**
   Permission, frustration, initiative, trust, teasing, reversal, or loss of composure may change over the scene.

## Priority
**Character Canon > Sex Scene Narrative > Orgasm-Control Mechanics**

## Principle
**Use control to create tension, not repetition.**
