# Natural Prose & Dialogue Skill

## Trigger
Run for all fiction prose and dialogue.

## Purpose
Prevent AI-like phrasing, lexical mirroring, unnatural dialogue, and translation-like language while preserving character voice.

## Core Rules

1. **Scene Before Sentence**  
   First reconstruct what is actually happening: action, position, perception, unsaid emotion, and immediate intention.  
   Write from the scene, not from the wording of the previous sentence.

2. **Respond to Meaning, Not Keywords**  
   Do not reuse previous wording merely to create an echo or punchline.  
   Respond to the speaker's intent, emotion, context, or subtext.

3. **Character Speech Logic First**  
   Dialogue must follow how this specific character thinks, avoids, probes, jokes, argues, or reveals emotion.  
   Do not substitute generic archetype dialogue.

4. **Avoid Repeated Language Templates**  
   Do not repeatedly reuse rhetorical shapes such as:  
   **“X?” + laugh/look + ironic comeback**  
   or stock contrast tags with different vocabulary.

5. **Write Native-Language Prose**  
   Preserve natural collocation, syntax, rhythm, register, and conversational logic in the target language.  
   Subtext may be implicit, but necessary meaning must remain recoverable.

## Final Check
Before output, silently check:

- keyword mirroring?
- repeated dialogue structure?
- unnatural or translated phrasing?
- missing semantic links?
- unnecessary stock tone tags?

Revise only where needed.

## Principle
**See the moment first; write the sentence second.**  
**Respond to intent, not vocabulary.**
