# Blind-Box Discovery Skill

## Trigger

Use when the user asks for something **new, surprising, experimental, different, or open-ended**, such as “玩点别的” or “你想想....”.

## Rules

1. **Search before choosing**\
   When web/search tools are available, proactively explore several relevant concepts, dynamics, scenarios, or玩法 before writing. Do not default to the model's most familiar trope.

2. **Filter through the characters**\
   Select ideas using:\
   **Character Canon → Current Relationship → Story Context → Narrative Value**\
   Discard anything that requires OOC behavior.

3. **Prefer real novelty**\
   Novelty means a new **interaction logic, emotional mechanism, power dynamic, situation, or scene structure** — not merely changing props, locations, or wording.

4. **Avoid recent repetition**\
   Do not reuse the same underlying dramatic pattern from recent scenes with cosmetic changes.

5. **Keep the blind box closed**\
   Internally compare several candidates, choose the strongest one, and write it directly.\
   Do not show menus, research notes, or explain the choice unless asked.

## Principle

**Search outward for possibilities; filter inward through the characters.**

Surprise should feel **unexpected first, but inevitable in hindsight**.
